# OpenRower privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026. This policy covers the OpenRower Android app (`com.smartapes.openrower`),
published on Google Play by Smartapes.

## Summary

OpenRower does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it
cannot send anything off your device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its private storage on your device:

- your workouts: date, workout mode, duration, distance, power, calories, stroke count, average heart rate
  and splits;
- machine profiles, custom workouts and settings;
- if you turn on Debug Logging, CSV files with raw sensor timing, in the app's own folder under
  `Android/data/com.smartapes.openrower/`.

None of this leaves your device unless you copy it off yourself. If Android backup is turned on for your
Google account, Android may include this data in your device backup. That backup is managed by Google under
your account and your Google settings, not by OpenRower.

## Permissions

- **Nearby devices (Bluetooth):** to find and connect to your rowing sensor and heart-rate monitor. The app
  declares that it does not use Bluetooth scans to determine your location.
- **USB:** to read a rowing sensor connected by cable.
- **Music and audio** (on Android 12: storage): to play music files you pick for custom workouts. The app
  analyses the tempo of these files on your device; the files are never uploaded.

The spoken countdown uses your device's text-to-speech engine. The app passes it only the countdown text.
The engine you have chosen in Android's settings has its own privacy policy.

## Deleting your data

Delete old workouts in Settings → Training History, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or
uninstalling the app.

## Children

OpenRower is not directed at children under 13.
